About Us

The Show

Welcome to Couch Lobsters, the film & series podcast made by amateurs for cinema enthusiasts.

The concept is beautifully simple: each episode, Jess and Dima assign each other a film or TV series to watch. Neither host tells the other what they think until the microphone is on. No spoiler warnings, no pre-discussion, no comparing notes beforehand.

What you get is two honest, unfiltered opinions that sometimes agree and sometimes really don't. Expect laughs, disagreements, deep dives, and the occasional very strong take about Fast & Furious.

⚠️ Fair warning: we discuss everything. All the twists, all the endings, all the details. Watch the film or series first. We'll be here when you're ready.
