The Concept

Each episode, Jess and Dima assign each other a film or series to watch. The twist? Both hosts keep their opinions completely secret until the microphone is on.

No pre-discussion. No spoiler-free zones. Pure, unfiltered takes from two amateurs who really love cinema.

02

The Warning

This podcast is proudly, unapologetically spoilerific. We discuss everything: endings, twists, hidden details, and all the moments you probably didn't expect.

Watch the films first. We'll be here when you're ready.

03

The Hosts

Jess and Dima are self-described amateurs who take their amateur status very seriously. Based in Australia, they bring unfiltered passion and the occasional heated disagreement to every episode.
